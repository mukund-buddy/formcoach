Surya Namaskar kaise karte hain? (12 steps)
  1. Pranamasana — seedhe khade ho, haath chest pe namaste
  2. Hasta Uttanasana — haath sar ke upar uthao, peeche jhuko
  3. Padahastasana — aage jhuko, haath pairon/zameen tak
  4. Ashwa Sanchalanasana — ek pair peeche, haath zameen, dekho upar
  5. Dandasana — dusra pair bhi peeche, seedhi plank
  6. Ashtanga Namaskara — ghutne, seena, matha zameen pe
  7. Bhujangasana — seena upar uthao (cobra)
  8. Adho Mukha Svanasana — kamar sabse upar, ulta V
  9. Ashwa Sanchalanasana — pehla pair aage lao
  10. Padahastasana — dusra pair bhi aage, aage jhuko
  11. Hasta Uttanasana — utho, haath upar
  12. Pranamasana — haath chest pe jodo. 1 round poora!
